Coach Guidance & Science

Protein is fixed at 2.0-2.2g per kg of bodyweight to prevent muscle catabolism during calorie deficits. Consume healthy unsaturated fats for hormonal synthesis and distribute carbohydrates around your pre- and post-workout windows for peak training output.

Measurement Instructions

Take measurements first thing in the morning before eating. Keep the tape snug against the skin without compressing soft tissue. For men, measure the neck below the larynx and waist at the level of the navel.

Progressive Overload Rule

For maximal strength gains, train in the 85-95% range (1-5 reps). For muscle hypertrophy (mass), focus on the 70-80% 1RM range (8-12 reps) with 2-3 RIR (Reps in Reserve).
